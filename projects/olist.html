Olist Delivery Risk Analysis

Delivery risk lives in the tail, not the average. The data points to reliability — keeping the promise date — rather than raw speed as the lever for customer satisfaction.

BigQuery SQL · Power BI · Git/GitHub

The average hides the tail

The median order arrives in 10 days. But the slowest 1% (~880 orders) took more than 46 days, and the worst took 209.

Key findings

Satisfaction drops off a cliff, not a slope

Arriving early barely beats arriving on time (4.3 vs 4.1). Crossing the promise date costs about 1.1 points.
