Equipment Tracking System for Endoscopy Service

Turning scattered repair records into a lookup tool I use in the field.

At a glance: 150+ devices · 40+ hospitals · 12 equipment categories · 3 linked tables

>

The problem:

Repair records at ETC Medical were spread across Excel files held by the admin team. When I was at a hospital and needed a device’s history, I had to ask and wait. That also meant no one could see patterns: which scopes failed most, and how often the same fault came back.

What I built:

An AppSheet app on a relational data model with three linked tables:

Table What it holds
Product records Each device by hospital, category, serial [?]
Equipment transitions Every movement: intake, inspection, repair, return
Contacts Hospital staff linked to each site

Records are connected by references, include computed fields, and every transition carries photos, so the data can be checked against the physical device.

Result

  • I look up any device’s history on-site in [? vài giây / thay vì bao lâu], without waiting for admin.
  • From the tracked data I analyzed equipment distribution by type, which showed which scope categories move most often.
  • The app is being extended with admin roles so the wider team can use it.
