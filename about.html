About me

From engineering physics to medical devices to business analysis.

My story

I studied Engineering Physics at HCMC University of Technology, where my thesis analyzed the brain's response to stimulus signals. In 2019, I started working with medical equipment as an Application Engineer, comparing product configurations against tender requirements and supporting product demos.

In 2021, while still working, I started an MBA in Management Consulting at FHNW. What draws me to business numbers is that they record what actually happened. In e-commerce, for example, each step of an order can be captured as data: when it shipped, when it arrived, and how much it cost. Following these records helps trace how the business really runs. I looked into several areas, and business analysis stood out because it connects data with problem-solving and data-driven decisions. From August 2022 to August 2025, I took a career break. During that time, I completed my MBA with a thesis on supply chain risk management for medical equipment distributors in Vietnam, and taught myself SQL, R and data analysis.

In September 2025, I joined ETC Medical as a Service Engineer. There, I built an AppSheet system to replace manual Excel tracking, covering 150+ devices across 40+ hospitals. In 2026, I explored my interest in business records further in my Olist delivery analysis. I am now looking for a Business Analyst role where I can combine data analysis with what I have learned from working with medical equipment.
